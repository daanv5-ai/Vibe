In the edit

Straight cuts on every phrase. Three exceptions: a tree crossing the lens at 0:21, the hyperlapse opening onto the lake at 1:11, and the jump to night at 1:39 while the poi is already spinning.

Words, small, only when the place changes: “bosco” at 0:21, “Lago Maggiore” at 1:11, “Trarego” at 1:46. Type those in CapCut. For the last second, a Grok Imagine title card that says Forcola, warm serif, on black, then lay it over 2:02–2:05.

Where to stand in Trarego

  • Top of Via Passo Piazza. Look back down the street. This is the roofs shot, 1:46.
  • San Martino, Via Passo Piazza. The stair, the portico, the tower. Shoot 1:53 on the way in and 2:00 from the open side of the square. The square is also the village balcony over the lake, so if that view is clear, it can be the road-and-lake hyperlapse.
  • Viale dei Martiri. The road through the upper village. Use it only if you need a second angle on the roofs. The town hall is beside the church, so it does not need its own flight.
  • Castelli di Cannero. Down at the shore, for 1:18. A separate stop from the village.

If people are on the church steps, use the Chiesa Vecchia in Viggiona for the last shot.
